Hiring remote developers from Latin America has become a key strategy for US companies looking to build strong tech teams. However, assessing talent across borders comes with unique challenges that traditional methods often fail to address. Skills testing offers a reliable way to evaluate candidates objectively, ensuring you make informed decisions while reducing bias.
