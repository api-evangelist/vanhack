# VanHack

> VanHack is a SaaS hiring platform that connects employers with pre-screened senior software engineers from a global community of over 500,000 vetted candidates ready to relocate or work remotely. It pairs expert human recruiters with Vanna, an AI recruiter that matches candidates from natural-language job descriptions, and coordinates interviews with calendar sync, Slack, and ATS integration. VanHack publishes no public developer API.
